MARS LANDER — ESP32 SENSOR AND STATUS WIRING
Board: ESP32 DevKit V1, ESP32-WROOM-32
This pin map records the retained sensors, display, and indicators only.
Verify board/module electrical requirements and physical wiring before power.

GPIO ASSIGNMENTS

Component                 ESP32 GPIO     Connection / Notes
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Green status LED          GPIO 25        Output with series resistor
Red status LED            GPIO 26        Output with series resistor
WS2812B data              GPIO 27        Verify logic-level requirements
BMP280 SDA                GPIO 21        I2C SDA
BMP280 SCL                GPIO 22        I2C SCL

DHT11 data                GPIO 17        Digital data input

SHARED I2C BUS
- SDA: GPIO 21; SCL: GPIO 22.
- Connect the LCD backpack and BMP280 to this bus; verify addresses differ.
- Check module logic voltage; do not assume every backpack is safe at 3.3V.

POWER AND ELECTRICAL RULES
- ESP32 GPIOs use 3.3V logic and are not 5V tolerant.
- Use suitable series resistors for the green and red indicator LEDs.
- GPIO pins are control signals and must not be used as peripheral power rails.
- Avoid separate power supplies unless electrical requirements strictly require one.
- Power low-current sensors (BMP280 < 1mA at 3.3V; DHT11 1-2.5mA at 3.3V/5V) directly from ESP32 dev-board power pins.
- 20x4 LCD backpack typically requires 5V VIN for backlight/contrast (~20-50mA); I2C SDA/SCL must remain 3.3V logic compatible.
- WS2812B (30 LEDs on GPIO 27): Full white draws up to 1.8A, but firmware clamps brightness to 60/255 (~150-300mA peak). Connect strip power to 5V/VIN (sharing common ground), never an ESP32 GPIO. Sizing of host USB supply should provide at least 1A-1.5A to handle ESP32 Wi-Fi bursts plus LEDs.
- Status LEDs (GPIO 25 green, GPIO 26 red) draw ~5-10mA through series current-limiting resistors.
- Share ground references across all peripherals and power supplies.
- The ESP32 needs a stable power source (USB or VIN) even though Wi-Fi removes its operational data cable to the Pi.
- GPIOs 6–11 are connected to flash on classic ESP32-WROOM-32 boards.
- GPIOs 34–39 are input-only; check boot-strapping behavior before use.

FIRMWARE BEHAVIOR
- Lander telemetry and status/mode commands use newline-delimited JSON over a
  Wi-Fi TCP connection initiated by the ESP32 to the Pi (default port 5006).
- USB serial remains available at 115200 8N1 for programming/debug only; the
  Pi does not open it.
- ACTIVE mode polls BMP280 and DHT11; failed readings are sent as null.
- STANDBY pauses sensor polling; GET_STATUS and mode commands remain available.
- LCD rotates lander status, BMP280 readings, DHT11 readings, and Wi-Fi link status.
- Green indicates ACTIVE; WS2812B retains the space-themed animation.
- The retired ramp, servos, physical button, and limit switches are not part of
  the lander wiring or firmware.
